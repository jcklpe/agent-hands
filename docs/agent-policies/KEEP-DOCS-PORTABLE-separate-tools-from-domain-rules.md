# Keep document tooling portable
Keep general Google Docs mechanics with the service capability and authentication with its setup owner. The capability must work without an organization-specific checkout, private vault or consumer meeting procedure. Domain rules and judgments stay with their consuming project; do not impose this repository's procedures on that project.

Preserve dry-run defaults, explicit execution, exact unique matches and revision guards. Actual shared-record edits require the consuming workflow's authorization; available tool capabilities do not alter that workflow's approval boundaries. Consumers may retain self-contained tooling where needed rather than acquire an unrelated provider or checkout dependency. The earlier extraction's small amount of implementation duplication does not grant an exception to write authorization or correctness guards.

## Enforcement and procedures
[Google Docs](../../use-google-docs/SKILL.md) and [Google setup](../../setup-google-services/SKILL.md) own the operational procedure and executable guards. This policy owns portability and permission boundaries, not a second editing walkthrough.
